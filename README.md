# DreamMapper 

Ever woken up from a dream so wild you HAD to tell someone, then forgot it 10 minutes later? Yeah. Same. That's why DreamMapper exists.

DreamMapper is a web journal for your wildest dreams. Write them down, watch them come to life, and actually remember the nonsense your brain cooks up at 3am.

## Features

- **Dream Journaling** - Log your dreams the moment you wake up, in a clean dark themed journal (your eyes are welcome)
- **AI Dream Images** - Your dreams, but visual. Results range from "beautiful" to "exactly as cursed as it felt"
- **AI Dream Videos** - Turn your dreams into moving videos. Relive the giant duck chase in full motion
- **AI Chatbot** - Talk about your dreams with an AI. Interpretations, recurring themes, or just someone to listen at 4am
- **Cloud Hosted** - Fully running on Cloudflare, fast and available anywhere

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend + Hosting:** Cloudflare (Pages/Workers)
- **Backend:** Database for users, dreams, and all your midnight ramblings
- **AI Backend:** Images, videos, and chatbot, running on a very expensive 2k server (my wallet is recovering), although it is currently hosted on HackClub AI temporarily.

## Security
